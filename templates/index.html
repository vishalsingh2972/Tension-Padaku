Hospital Pharmacy & Medicine Guidance

Room 50, Ground Floor

Pharmacy Location: Room 50 (Ground Floor)

Free and subsidized government generic medicines are dispensed at Room 50 for registered outpatients and inpatients.

Important Pharmacy Guidelines:

  • ✓ Valid Doctor Prescription: Always carry your stamped GGH Kurnool doctor slip.
  • ✓ Dosage Instructions: Verify medicine names, dosage times, and precautions with the pharmacist before leaving the counter.
  • ✓ Emergency Medicines: Available 24/7 through the casualty counter in Room 5.

OPD Registration & Token Process

How to consult doctors at GGH Kurnool
1
Arrival & Registration Counter

Visit the Main Entrance Ground Floor registration counter to generate your OPD consultation token.

2
Aarogyasri / Ayushman Bharat Desk

Patients with government health cards can authenticate their cards at the dedicated help desk.

3
Proceed to Assigned Room

Take your token slip directly to the department room (e.g. Room 12 for General Medicine or Room 25 for Cardiology).

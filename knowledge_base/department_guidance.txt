GOVERNMENT GENERAL HOSPITAL, KURNOOL
GENERAL DEPARTMENT GUIDANCE

This document provides general guidance for
hospital navigation and health-related questions.

IMPORTANT:
This is general informational guidance only.
It is not a medical diagnosis.


HEADACHE:

For a headache, General Medicine may be appropriate
for an initial medical evaluation.

General Medicine:
Room 12


CHEST-RELATED SYMPTOMS:

For chest-related symptoms, Cardiology may be relevant
for medical evaluation.

Cardiology:
Room 25


EAR, NOSE OR THROAT PROBLEMS:

For ear, nose, or throat-related problems, ENT may be
relevant.

ENT:
Room 18


BONE OR JOINT PROBLEMS:

For bone, joint, or musculoskeletal problems,
Orthopaedics may be relevant.

Orthopaedics:
Room 30


DENTAL PROBLEMS:

For dental-related problems, dental-related services
are mentioned in the available hospital records.


PHYSIOTHERAPY:

Physiotherapy services are mentioned in the available
hospital records.


ECG:

ECG services are mentioned in the available
hospital records.

ECG:
Room 46


LABORATORY:

Laboratory services are mentioned in the available
hospital records.

Laboratory:
Room 45


PHARMACY:

Pharmacy-related services are mentioned in the
available hospital records.

Pharmacy:
Room 50


EMERGENCY:

For potentially life-threatening or rapidly worsening
symptoms, the user should seek immediate emergency
medical care.

Emergency:
Room 5
